Detailed Description

A replacement for the EntryExitComponent that computes the entry and exit points analytically in the fragment shader, by intersecting the camera ray of each fragment with a parallelepiped or a sphere. No entry and exit point images are needed.

The geometry is defined in a selectable coordinate space of the volume (Data, Model, or World). In Data space the volume occupies the unit cube, hence the default parallelepiped (origin 0 and unit edges) reproduces the volume bounding box. Optionally, the geometry is also clipped against the volume bounding box, and it is always clipped against the near and far planes of the camera.

Like the EntryExitComponent, the setup segment defines entryPoint, exitPoint, entryPointDepth, exitPointDepth, rayLength, and rayDirection. The surfaceNormal sampler and useSurfaceNormals are declared for compatibility with other components, but useSurfaceNormals is always false. Requires a camera uniform named by cameraName.
